01 — Manifesto

I design interfaces the way I play chess and sketch — by reading the whole board first, then obsessing over a single square. My background in AI taught me how systems think. Design taught me how people feel. The best products live exactly there, in that gap.

Method Research → model → prototype → ship. I like the messy middle.
Belief Clarity is a design material. So is restraint.
Curiosity Neural nets, endgames, ink wash, and everything in between.
